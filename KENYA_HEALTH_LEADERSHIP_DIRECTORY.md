# Kenya Health Leadership & Strategic Outreach Directory 🇰🇪
## Comprehensive Stakeholder Contacts, Institutional Dossiers & Outreach Playbook

This directory compiles verified health leadership, county coordinators, hospital superintendents, faith-based networks, and donor partners across Kenya. It provides exact physical locations, leadership titles, institutional mandates, and ready-to-dispatch outreach packages tailored to Kenya's devolved healthcare ecosystem.

## 1. County Health Management Teams (Devolved Healthcare Authorities)

In Kenya, healthcare is 100% devolved to the 47 County Governments. Engagement begins with the **County Executive Committee Member (CEC) for Health** and the **County Reproductive Health Coordinator (CRHC)**.

### Priority 1: Garissa County
* **Department**: Department of Health Services, County Government of Garissa
* **Physical Office**: Garissa County Headquarters, Kismayu Road, Garissa
* **Key Facilities**: Garissa County Referral Hospital (Level 5), Modogashe Sub-County Hospital, Masalani Sub-County Hospital, Dadaab Sub-County Hospital
* **Maternal Mortality Context**: MMR $\approx 646$ per 100,000 live births (KDHS). Long transfer corridors (up to 150 km) from desert dispensaries result in mothers arriving in eclamptic convulsions or shock.
* **Key Targets**:
  - CEC Member for Health Services
  - Chief Officer of Health & Sanitation
  - County Reproductive Health Coordinator (CRHC)
* **Outreach Pitch**: Zero-toll Somali & Swahili danger sign triage; live bed/theatre visibility connecting Modogashe and Masalani dispensaries to Garissa Level 5 hospital.

### Priority 2: Kilifi County
* **Department**: Kilifi County Department of Health Services
* **Physical Office**: Kilifi County Health Headquarters, Hospital Road, Kilifi Town
* **Key Facilities**: Kilifi County Referral Hospital (Level 5), Malindi Sub-County Hospital (Level 4), Mariakani Sub-County Hospital, Bamba Sub-County Hospital
* **Maternal Mortality Context**: High prevalence of teenage pregnancy and home deliveries in hinterland areas (Bamba, Ganze, Magarini); delayed transfers result in fatal postpartum hemorrhage.
* **Key Targets**:
  - CEC Member for Health Services
  - County Director of Health
  - County Reproductive Health Coordinator
* **Outreach Pitch**: Community Health Promoter (CHP) danger-sign telemetry; automated hospital handshake eliminating blind transfers to Kilifi and Malindi hospitals.

## 2. National Ministry of Health & Regulatory Authorities

### 1. Division of Reproductive & Maternal Health (DRMH)
* **Location**: Ministry of Health, Afya House, Cathedral Road, Nairobi
* **Leadership**: Head, Division of Reproductive and Maternal Health
* **Mandate**: Oversees national maternal health clinical guidelines, National MPDSR Secretariat, and MOH 711 reproductive health indicator reporting.
* **Strategic Objective**: Align SEQOY's MPDSR analytics with national surveillance standards and SDG 3.1 targets.

### 2. Digital Health Agency (DHA)
* **Location**: Afya House, Cathedral Road, Nairobi
* **Mandate**: Established under the **Kenya Digital Health Act 2023** to govern the National Health Information Exchange (HIE), electronic health standards, and data interoperability.
* **Strategic Objective**: Verify SEQOY’s HL7 FHIR R4 ServiceRequest and Observation bundle architecture for national HIE sandbox accreditation.

### 3. Social Health Authority (SHA / SHIF)
* **Location**: Community, Ragati Road, Upper Hill, Nairobi
* **Mandate**: Successor to NHIF, administering the Primary Healthcare Fund and Emergency, Chronic & Critical Illness Fund.
* **Strategic Objective**: Validate SEQOY’s timestamped referral audit logs for automated SHA emergency maternal transfer reimbursement claims.

## 3. Faith-Based Health Networks (Strike-Resilient Referral Corridors)

Faith-based and mission facilities deliver **~40% of maternal care in Kenya** and remain open 24/7 during public hospital doctor strikes (KMPDU/KUCO).

### 1. Christian Health Association of Kenya (CHAK)
* **Headquarters**: Musa Gitau Road, Off Waiyaki Way, Lavington, Nairobi
* **Leadership**: General Secretary / Health Programs Director
* **Network**: 550+ mission hospitals, health centres, and dispensaries across Kenya.
* **Anchor Facilities**:
  - **AIC Kijabe Hospital** (Kiambu / Rift Valley corridor)
  - **Tenwek Hospital** (Bomet / South Rift corridor)
  - **PCEA Chogoria Hospital** (Tharaka Nithi / Mt. Kenya)
  - **Friends Hospital Kaimosi** (Vihiga / Western)
* **Partnership Model**: Deploy SEQOY as the emergency maternal referral and bed-tracking network linking CHAK member hospitals to surrounding rural dispensaries during public sector service interruptions.

### 2. Kenya Conference of Catholic Bishops (KCCB) — Catholic Health Commission
* **Headquarters**: Waumini House, Westlands, Nairobi
* **Leadership**: National Executive Secretary, Commission for Health
* **Network**: Over 450 Catholic-run hospitals and dispensaries across 26 dioceses.
* **Anchor Facilities**:
  - **Consolata Hospital Nyeri**
  - **St. Luke’s Hospital Kaloleni** (Kilifi)
  - **Our Lady of Lourdes Mwea Hospital** (Kirinyaga)
* **Partnership Model**: Zero-cost manual outreach tool for Catholic parish dispensaries and Community Health Units to track high-risk pregnancies and coordinate emergency admissions.
